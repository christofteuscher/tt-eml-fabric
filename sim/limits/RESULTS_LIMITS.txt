============================================================================================
PART 1  THE DERIVATION:  why bias current cancels out of energy per evaluation
============================================================================================

A translinear cell is a current-mode circuit held at its operating point by servo
amplifiers.  Two facts fix its energy per evaluation:

  (a) POWER is proportional to bias current.   MEASURED, POWER.md 2.3:
      P_vdd = 204.4 * pbias[uA] - 2.9 uW  over an 8:1 range, i.e. P = k_I * I.
      Every leg is a fixed-ratio mirror of pbias, so k_I = V_dd * (I_vdd/I_pbias).

  (b) SETTLING TIME is proportional to C / gm, and in the subthreshold /
      translinear regime gm = I_branch / (n V_T).   Therefore

          t_settle = N_tau * C / gm = N_tau * C * n V_T / I_branch    ~  1/I.

  Multiply:   E = P * t = [V_dd * I_vdd] * [N_tau * C * n V_T / I_branch]

          +--------------------------------------------------------+
          |   E_op  =  F * N_tau * C * V_dd * n * V_T               |
          |   with  F = I_vdd / I_branch   (the scaffolding fanout) |
          +--------------------------------------------------------+

  The bias current CANCELS EXACTLY.  Not approximately -- it appears once in the
  numerator (power) and once in the denominator (bandwidth) with the same exponent.
  Scaling the whole fabric to nanoamps buys nothing: it buys proportionally less
  power and proportionally more time.  Note the form is C*V_dd*(n V_T), NOT C*V^2:
  analog settling is exponential, so the second voltage is the thermal voltage times
  the number of e-folds you demand, not the supply rail.

  FORWARD PREDICTION from device physics, nothing fitted:
    C  = CC_OT (Miller comp. on the exp servo)          = 2.00 pF
    I_branch = I_tail/2 (XMT_OT input pair)             = 1.980 uA
    gm = I_branch/(n V_T), n=1.3, V_T=25.85 mV          = 58.9 uS
    tau = C/gm                             PREDICTED    = 33.9 ns
    tau                                    MEASURED     = 45.9 ns  (POWER.md 3.1, 38-57 ns range)
    ratio predicted/measured = 0.74  -- a forward prediction good to 26 %

    F = I_vdd / I_branch                                = 15.1  (scaffolding fanout)
    N_tau = t_1%/tau                                    = 8.8 e-folds
    E = F * N_tau * C * V_dd * n V_T       PREDICTED    = 29.3 pJ
    E = P_cell * t_1% (post-layout)        MEASURED     = 39.6 pJ  (POWER.md 6)
    ratio = 0.74  -> THE DERIVATION IS CONFIRMED to 26 %

============================================================================================
PART 1b  DIRECT TEST OF BIAS INVARIANCE  (NEW MEASUREMENT, bias_sweep.py)
============================================================================================

POWER.md 2.3 measured P vs pbias.  Nobody had measured t_settle vs pbias, so E(I) was
never tested.  bias_sweep.py does it: same netlist, same die context, same normalised
operating point and same full-scale exp step at 4 bias points over an 8:1 range,
tt/27C, gear/maxord=2, 2 ns grid.  MEASURED:

  pbias/uA     I_vdd/uA      P/uW    t1%/ns   tau/ns     E1%/pJ
  0.125          7.1346    23.544     821.0    121.0      19.33
  0.250         14.5270    47.939     458.9     61.0      22.00
  0.500         29.5979    97.673     342.9     45.9      33.49   <-- as shipped
  1.000         60.2693   198.889     342.8     49.2      68.17

  Piecewise log-log exponents (E ~ I^p):

    bias range        tau exponent      E exponent     regime
    0.125 -> 0.25 uA     -0.99            +0.19        SUBTHRESHOLD: hypothesis HOLDS
    0.25  -> 0.50 uA     -0.41            +0.61        knee
    0.50  -> 1.00 uA     +0.10            +1.03        RC-LIMITED: hypothesis FAILS

  THE HYPOTHESIS IS CONFIRMED EXACTLY -- BUT ONLY BELOW A KNEE, AND THE SHIPPED PART
  SITS ON THE WRONG SIDE OF IT.

  * Below 250 nA the cancellation is essentially perfect: tau ~ I^-0.99 against the
    predicted I^-1.0, and energy moves only 19.33 -> 22.00 pJ (I^+0.19) for a 2x change
    in bias.  Scaling to nanoamps buys nothing, exactly as predicted.
  * Above 500 nA the cancellation BREAKS: tau stops falling (+0.10, i.e. flat) and energy
    becomes strictly proportional to bias current (I^+1.03).  Extra current buys zero
    speed and costs proportional power.  This is pure waste.
  * WHY: a current-INDEPENDENT pole takes over.  The exp path runs through RU
    (res_high_po, L=45.76, = 51.5 kohm, POWER.md 3.4) into CFU = 1 pF.
    RU * CFU = 51.5 ns -- and the measured tau floors at 45.9-49.2 ns.  Once C/gm falls
    below the fixed RC of the signal path, gm no longer sets the bandwidth and the
    denominator of E = P*t stops tracking I.  The floor is a RESISTOR, not a transistor.
  * CONSEQUENCE: the shipped 500 nA bias is at or past the knee.  The cell would be
    1.73x CHEAPER per evaluation at 125 nA (19.33 vs 33.49 pJ) at the price of 2.4x
    latency -- and POWER.md 2.3 notes bias current is the paper's accuracy lever, so
    that saving is paid for in bits.  Even so: 19.33 pJ is still 1.4x a whole
    13.8 pJ digital datapath, for ONE of the seven cells the task needs.
  * So the practical statement is stronger than the hypothesis, not weaker:
    THERE IS NO BIAS CURRENT AT WHICH THIS FABRIC IS CHEAP.  Lowering bias hits a
    floor of ~19 pJ/cell; raising it is strictly wasted energy.  The lever does not exist.

============================================================================================
PART 2  IS THE FLOOR SET BY kT/C NOISE?   -- the hypothesis fails here
============================================================================================

The hypothesis says C is bounded below by kT/C noise at the required SNR, giving a
hard thermodynamic floor.  Evaluate it: a sampled node with swing V_sig has
v_n^2 = kT/C, so SNR = 2^b demands  C >= kT * 2^(2b) / V_sig^2.

  bits       C_min (kT/C)   vs CC_OT=2pF E floor if C=C_min
  2.28       9.770e-20 F       4.9e-08     1.432e-18 J
  4.30       1.607e-18 F       8.0e-07     2.355e-17 J
  8.00       2.714e-16 F       1.4e-04     3.977e-15 J
  10.00      4.343e-15 F       2.2e-03     6.364e-14 J
  12.00      6.949e-14 F       3.5e-02     1.018e-12 J
  14.00      1.112e-12 F       5.6e-01     1.629e-11 J
  16.00      1.779e-11 F       8.9e+00     2.607e-10 J
  18.00      2.846e-10 F       1.4e+02     4.171e-09 J

  VERDICT: THE kT/C HALF OF THE HYPOTHESIS IS WRONG, AND WRONG BY 6 ORDERS OF MAGNITUDE.
  At the fabric's actual precision (2.28-4.30 bits) kT/C permits C = 1.6e-18 F -- ATTOFARADS.
  The 2 pF that is actually there is 1e+06 times larger than noise requires.
  kT/C does not bind until b = 14.4 bits with a 1 V swing.

  So what DOES set C?  Three things, all architectural, none thermodynamic:
    1. STABILITY.  CC_OT = 2 pF is a Miller compensation capacitor.  It exists to give
       the servo loop phase margin.  Its size is set by the loop's non-dominant pole,
       not by noise.  PEX_CHAIN/PEX_SHIPPED record that the BUFFERED variant rang in
       15 of 25 corners; the shipped cell is stable, and 2 pF is what bought that.
    2. PARASITICS.  The extracted cell carries 1773 fF over 1317 capacitors
       (PEX_SHIPPED.md 1) before any deliberate cap, and parasitics cost +19 %% of
       settling time (POWER.md 6).  That is a lithographic floor, not a noise floor.
    3. MATCHING, not noise, sets the precision.  FAB_ERROR.md measures 1.288 u of
       swing against 0.266 u of LEVEL SIGMA = 2.28 bits.  That sigma is device
       mismatch and servo offset -- static, uncorrelated with bandwidth, and NOT
       reducible by spending charge.  A 1.88 %% ln-slope mismatch caps the root at
       5.73 bits and "no offset trim removes" it.  The fabric is mismatch-limited,
       not noise-limited, so the kT/C argument never engages.

============================================================================================
PART 3  THE REAL FLOOR, AND HOW FAR ABOVE IT THE FABRIC SITS
============================================================================================

The correct fundamental floor for a current-mode translinear circuit is SHOT noise on
the delivered charge, not kT/C on a stored voltage.  Deliver charge Q = I_sig * t through
a junction; Poisson statistics give sigma_Q = sqrt(q Q), so SNR = sqrt(Q/q).  Hence

        Q_min = q * 4^b      and      E_min = V_swing * q * 4^b.

This is Sarpeshkar's scaling: analog cost grows as 4^b (2 bits per 4x charge), while a
digital datapath's cost grows roughly LINEARLY in bit width for adders and quadratically
for multipliers -- i.e. polynomially in b, against the analog exponential.  That is
exactly why he concludes analog is more efficient at LOW precision and digital wins at
HIGH precision, with the crossover at intermediate precision (his stated figure is of
order 8-10 bits for the technologies he analysed; the qualitative claim -- exponential
vs polynomial in b -- is the load-bearing part and is reproduced below).

  bits     E_min shot (J)     E_dig130 (J)   analog/digital
  2.28       3.779e-18       3.554e-12          1.1e-06
  4.30       6.217e-17       6.547e-12          9.5e-06
  8.00       1.050e-14       1.382e-11          7.6e-04
  10.00      1.680e-13       1.884e-11          8.9e-03
  12.00      2.688e-12       2.467e-11          1.1e-01
  13.00      1.075e-11       2.789e-11          3.9e-01
  14.00      4.301e-11       3.131e-11          1.4e+00
  16.00      6.881e-10       3.878e-11          1.8e+01

  CROSSOVER (fundamental analog floor = this 130 nm digital datapath): b* = 13.8 bits
  Sarpeshkar's regime claim is REPRODUCED: below b* analog is fundamentally cheaper.
  The fabric operates at 2.28-4.30 bits -- 9 to 11 bits INSIDE the analog-wins regime.

  AND YET IT LOSES.  Quantify the gap:
    fundamental shot-noise floor at 4.30 bits          = 6.22e-17 J  (0.062 fJ)
    what one cell actually costs (POWER.md 6)         = 3.96e-11 J  (39.6 pJ)
    ratio                                             = 6.4e+05
    equivalently: the cell spends the charge of a 13.9-bit computation to deliver 4.30 bits.

============================================================================================
PART 4  THE RECONCILIATION:  what would it cost if the scaffolding were free?
============================================================================================

The measured asymmetry (POWER.md 1.1 / FAB_AREA.md): the four bipolar junctions that
actually evaluate eml are 2.7 % of cell area and draw 0 % DIRECTLY from the 3.3 V rail
(13.3 % of supply current, at 1.2 V collectors).  The four servo amplifiers that exist
only to hold their operating points are 42.5 % of area and 74.5 % of supply current.

Price the bare translinear core.  It carries I_core = 3.962 uA at V_core = 1.2 V.  Its own
capacitance is junction + local wiring only -- no Miller compensation, because with no
servo loop there is no loop to compensate.  Take C_core from the extracted total scaled
by the core's area share, a deliberately PESSIMISTIC estimate (the core is resistors and
NPNs, which are not the capacitive part of the cell).

    C_core = C_pex * 2.7 %                 = 47.9 fF
    gm_core = I_c / V_T  (BJT, n = 1)     = 153.3 uS
    tau_core                              = 0.31 ns   (vs 45.9 ns for the servoed cell)
    t_settle = 8.8 tau                    = 2.7 ns   (vs 402 ns measured)
    E_core = V_core * I_core * t_settle   = 13.0 fJ   (vs 39624 fJ measured)

    ---> THE SCAFFOLDING TAX IS 3046x IN ENERGY PER CELL.
         (compare: 16x in area, 5.6x in supply current -- energy is the harshest of the three)

  Does the scaffolding-free fabric beat digital?  Use the same 7-cell, 4.0-bit task
  that the matched comparison used (energy_area_compare.py CASE 1):
    fabric as built,   N=7 d=3        =    420.8 pJ
    digital datapath, matched 4.0 b   =    13.82 pJ   -> fabric loses 30x  [PRIOR RESULT, reproduced]
    7 bare translinear cores          =   0.0910 pJ   -> IDEAL CORE BEATS DIGITAL BY 152x

  So Sarpeshkar is not contradicted -- he is CONFIRMED, on this very circuit.  The
  primitive is 152x cheaper than the digital datapath at 4 bits, exactly as his argument
  predicts for the low-precision regime.  The fabric loses anyway because it pays a
  3046x tax to make that primitive addressable, composable and stable.

  THE CENTRAL RESULT:  a single-primitive analog fabric pays a COMPOSABILITY OVERHEAD
  that swamps the primitive's efficiency.  The overhead is not a defect of this layout;
  it is what the primitive needs in order to be a fabric element rather than a curiosity:
    - servo amplifiers, because a translinear loop only holds its law if its collector
      voltages are pinned (42.5 % of area, 74.5 % of current, and they set F = 15.1);
    - Miller caps, because the servos are feedback loops that must not ring
      (2 pF each, and C is the OTHER term in E = F*N_tau*C*V_dd*n*V_T);
    - weight DACs and config flops, because a fabric must be programmable
      (27.4 % of fabric area, FAB_AREA.md; and the LINK costs 88 % of a cell in power
      against 28 % in area, POWER.md 4.2).
  Every one of those is a consequence of composability, not of the exp/ln primitive.
  Both terms in the energy law -- F and C -- are scaffolding terms.  The primitive
  contributes NEITHER.

  At what precision would the fabric AS BUILT beat digital?  Solve E_fab(N=7) = E_dig(b):
    b = 60.2 bits.  The fabric delivers 4.30.  It would have to be 56 bits more accurate
    than it is, in a design whose mismatch caps it at 5.73 bits (FAB_ERROR.md).  Unreachable.

============================================================================================
PART 5  WHERE COULD IT WIN?  Each regime as an inequality, then evaluated.
============================================================================================

[1] VERY LOW PRECISION (< 4 bits): digital still pays a full datapath
    condition : E_fab(1 cell) < E_dig(b)   for b < 4
    evaluated : E_fab = 39.6 pJ (ONE cell, the indivisible quantum)  vs  E_dig(2 b) = 3.178 pJ
    VERDICT   : FAIL -- digital gets CHEAPER as precision falls (a 4-entry LUT is nearly free) while the
                fabric does not: 98.57 uW and 402 ns are fixed costs paid at ANY precision.
                Analog has no sub-linear low-precision mode here. Loses by 12x.

[2] ALWAYS-ON CONTINUOUS-TIME MONITORING (neither side can duty-cycle)
    condition : P_fab < E_dig * f_required   AND   f_required <= 1/t_settle (feasibility)
    evaluated : f_break-even = P_fab/E_dig = 7.1 MHz (1 cell) / 86.4 MHz (N=7)  vs  f_max = 1/t_settle = 2.49 MHz (1 cell) / 2.84 MHz (N=7)
    VERDICT   : FAIL -- the two conditions are MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE. The fabric must be re-evaluated
                2.9x (1 cell) to 30x (N=7) faster than it can physically settle. Its own
                bandwidth ceiling closes the door its static power opens. This is structural:
                E = P*t means break-even rate = 1/t_dig, and t_dig << t_analog always.

[3] ANALOG-IN / ANALOG-OUT: the fabric skips conversion the digital path must pay
    condition : n_ports * E_conv(b_sensor) > E_fab - E_dig
    evaluated : deficit to make up = 407 pJ;  E_conv = 34.0 fJ/conv-step x 2^b
                b_sensor= 4 b: E_conv=    0.54 pJ -> need   748.1 conversions/eval
                b_sensor= 8 b: E_conv=    8.70 pJ -> need    46.8 conversions/eval
                b_sensor=10 b: E_conv=   34.82 pJ -> need    11.7 conversions/eval
                b_sensor=12 b: E_conv=  139.26 pJ -> need     2.9 conversions/eval
                a 7-cell fabric HAS AT MOST 14 input ports (2 per cell, FAB_AREA 1.2)
    VERDICT   : FAIL -- at a FAIR converter width (the fabric answers to 4.3 bits, so a
                4-6 bit converter is what the digital path needs) you would need 187 to 748
                conversions per evaluation against 14 available ports: short by 13-53x.
                It only closes if you grant the digital side 10-12 bit converters it does
                not need (12-3 conversions), which is not a fair comparison.

[4] EXPENSIVE TRANSCENDENTALS (CORDIC: ~1 iteration per bit)
    condition : E_fab(1 cell) < 2 * b * E_cordic_iter    [one eml cell = exp AND ln, so 2 CORDICs]
    evaluated : E_fab = 39.6 pJ (ONE cell)
                b= 2.28: 2 CORDICs =    19.0 pJ  -> fabric loses by 2.09x
                b= 3.00: 2 CORDICs =    25.0 pJ  -> fabric loses by 1.59x
                b= 4.00: 2 CORDICs =    33.3 pJ  -> fabric loses by 1.19x
                b= 4.30: 2 CORDICs =    35.8 pJ  -> fabric loses by 1.11x
                b= 6.00: 2 CORDICs =    49.9 pJ  -> fabric WINS by 1.26x
                b= 8.00: 2 CORDICs =    66.6 pJ  -> fabric WINS by 1.68x
                b=16.00: 2 CORDICs =   133.1 pJ  -> fabric WINS by 3.36x
    crossover : b* = 4.76 bits.  The fabric delivers 4.30 at a tree root -- it MISSES the
                crossover by 0.46 bits and loses by 1.11x, its narrowest defeat anywhere.
    VERDICT   : FAIL, but this is the CLOSEST regime and it is worth stating precisely why.
                (i) The margin is only 1.11x -- inside the +-19 % parasitic and +-4.6 % corner
                     spread, so this regime is a genuine near-miss rather than a rout.
                (ii) It vanishes the instant digital is allowed a LUT instead of CORDIC:
                     a 16-entry exp LUT + interpolation costs 4.85 pJ, and the fabric loses 8x.
                     NOBODY COMPUTES A 4-BIT EXPONENTIAL WITH CORDIC. At 4 bits a LUT has 16
                     entries. CORDIC is only the right baseline at >=12 bits -- precisely where
                     the fabric cannot go (mismatch caps it at 5.73 bits, FAB_ERROR.md).
                (iii) It holds for ONE cell only. Any real function needs a tree, and each link
                     costs 85.64 uW = 88 % of a cell (POWER.md 4.2), so N=7 loses 12x.

[5] HIGH-DIMENSIONAL INTERPOLATION (LUT cost N^d)
    condition : E_fab < min( E_LUT(N^d), E_closedform, E_MLP )
    evaluated : d=9, 8b axes -> 256^9 = 4.7e+21 bytes: LUT INFEASIBLE, as claimed.
                BUT the digital escape hatch is not a LUT, it is the closed form:
                I.9.18 (9 vars) as 9 mul + 5 add = 88.7 pJ, exact to 8 bits.
                fabric N=32 d=4 = 2474 pJ at 4.7 bits (IDEAL hw) -> loses 28x.
    VERDICT   : FAIL -- the LUT^d blow-up is real but it is a straw man: digital only
                uses a LUT when no closed form exists, and when none exists the comparison
                is against an MLP, where PRIOR WORK ALREADY MEASURED the fabric losing
                7-15x on ACCURACY at matched parameter count. Both exits are closed.

[6] [OWN] A SCAFFOLDING-FREE SUCCESSOR -- the only regime the derivation actually opens
    condition : E = F*N_tau*C*V_dd*n*V_T  <  E_dig.  Three levers, all architectural.
    evaluated : as built  F=15.1, C=2000 fF, V_dd=3.3 V   -> 39.6 pJ/cell
                open-loop core, no servo    F= 1.0 C=   48 fF V=1.2 ->   0.0130 pJ/cell (7 cells:   0.091 pJ, vs digital 13.8 pJ)
                + 1.2 V rail                F= 1.0 C=   48 fF V=1.2 ->   0.0130 pJ/cell (7 cells:   0.091 pJ, vs digital 13.8 pJ)
                F=3 (partial servo), 100 fF F= 3.0 C=  100 fF V=1.8 ->   0.1589 pJ/cell (7 cells:   1.113 pJ, vs digital 13.8 pJ)
                F=15 but C=100 fF (no comp) F=15.1 C=  100 fF V=3.3 ->   1.4653 pJ/cell (7 cells:  10.257 pJ, vs digital 13.8 pJ)
    VERDICT   : WIN, by up to 152x -- BUT the levers are exactly the composability
                machinery. F=1 means no servo, so collector voltages float and the
                translinear law fails (POWER.md: the ln port IS a servo-held node).
                C=50 fF means no Miller compensation, and PEX_SHIPPED records the
                uncompensated variant RINGING in 15 of 25 corners. The win is available
                only to a circuit that is no longer a programmable fabric.

SUMMARY OF REGIME TESTS
============================================================================================
  [1] VERY LOW PRECISION (< 4 bits): digital still pays a full datapath FAIL
  [2] ALWAYS-ON CONTINUOUS-TIME MONITORING (neither side can duty-cycle) FAIL
  [3] ANALOG-IN/ANALOG-OUT                          FAIL
  [4] EXPENSIVE TRANSCENDENTALS (CORDIC)            FAIL
  [5] HIGH-DIM INTERPOLATION                        FAIL
  [6] SCAFFOLDING-FREE SUCCESSOR                    WIN

  5 of 6 regimes fail on measured numbers. The 6th wins only by deleting the scaffolding,
  which is the same as saying: THE PRIMITIVE WINS, THE FABRIC LOSES, AND THE DIFFERENCE
  BETWEEN THEM IS THE PAPER. The fabric's energy law contains no term belonging to eml.
